## Why it wins

1. **Subject (most important):** A quiet, earned moment of pride — a mechanic who has fought a stubborn engine for three days finally gets it to catch and hold. No trophy, podium, graduation, medal, applause, or congratulation; the pride is entirely in the work landing.
2. **Concrete scene:** Split knuckles on the last bolt, a gasket seating on the second try, three days of effort, an engine that catches and holds — every detail is physical and visible, so the moment is seen happening rather than summarised.
3. **Earned pride:** The difficulty is felt in the body (split knuckles) and in the retries (second try, three days); nothing is asserted or explained, the pride is inferred from the struggle and the result.
4. **One read, lands on the last word:** The line runs in a single breath and the pride arrives on "holds" — the engine finally staying running — with no setup the reader has to assemble.
5. **Freshness:** An unglamorous garage scene with split knuckles and a gasket is not a stock image; it avoids the trophy/podium/graduation clichés.
